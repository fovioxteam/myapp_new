# Privacy Policy for Foviox

**Last updated: August 11, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Foviox. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
When you sign up using Google or Apple, we collect:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your profile picture (avatar)
- Your Apple or Google user ID

### 2.2 Content You Create
We collect and store:
- Photos and videos you upload
- Captions and text you write
- Tags and links you add to posts
- Comments you make
- Likes and saves

### 2.3 Usage Data
We automatically collect:
- How you interact with the app
- What posts you view
- How long you use the app
- Device information (model, OS version)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the app
- Show your posts to other users
- Allow you to add LINK TAGS to photos (unique feature!)
- Recommend content you might like
- Improve the app experience
- Send notifications (if you allow)

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Public Content
Your posts, photos, videos, and link tags are visible to other users of Foviox.

### 4.2 Third-Party Services
We use Firebase (Google) for:
- Authentication (Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In)
- Database (Cloud Firestore)
- Storage (Firebase Storage)
- Analytics
- Push Notifications

### 4.3 External Links
When you add a link tag to your post, users can click it to visit external websites. We are not responsible for those websites' privacy practices.

## 5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures:
- Encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS)
- Secure Firebase infrastructure
- Regular security updates
- Limited access to user data

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Opt out of certain data collection

To exercise these rights, contact us at: **fovioxteam@gmail.com**

## 7. Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove:
- Your posts
- Your photos and videos
- Your comments and likes
- Your profile information

Some data may remain in backups for a limited time.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Foviox is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and within the app.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

**Email:** fovioxteam@gmail.com  
**App:** Foviox Social Network

## 💡 How Foviox Stands Out

**Unique Feature:** Foviox allows users to add interactive LINK TAGS to photos and videos. When you tap on a tag, it opens a link to external websites, products, or apps. This feature sets Foviox apart from other social networks and makes it perfect for:

- **Shopping:** Tag products in your photos
- **Travel:** Link to destinations
- **Food:** Share recipes and restaurants
- **Entertainment:** Link to music, videos, and events

All external links are filtered through a whitelist to ensure user safety.
